QUESTIONS

Common questions

Will the content sound like it was written by AI? +

No. The first week of every engagement is spent building a bespoke brand voice guide and prompt library trained on your clients' existing content. The output matches their best copywriter — because we've built the system around their actual language, tone, and style. Most clients can't tell the difference.

We already use ChatGPT. How is this different? +

Tools are one thing. A system is another. You're not getting access to AI tools — you're getting a fully built, maintained, and managed AI production department. Custom prompt libraries, brand voice guides, weekly delivery workflows, QA, and ongoing refinement. Anyone can use a hammer. We build the house.

How long before we see results? +

Your first content batch is delivered in week two. Most agencies report meaningful time savings from week three onward, once the system is calibrated and the revision cycle settles. By month two, the system is running at full capacity and your team is noticeably lighter on production overhead.

What if the content needs revisions? +

Each deliverable includes two revision rounds. We treat month one as a calibration period — expect one or two cycles as we dial in the voice guides. After that, first-pass approval rates typically exceed 90%. Revisions are handled within 24 hours on the Starter plan, same day on Full-service.

What's the minimum commitment? +

Six months, billed monthly. We ask for six months because the first month is setup and calibration, and the real ROI compounds from month two onward. Termination requires 30 days' written notice after the initial term. Payment is due upfront each month before work begins.
